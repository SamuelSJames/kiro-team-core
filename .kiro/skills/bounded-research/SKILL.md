# Bounded Research

## Owner
Agent 13 — Research Agent.

## Shared Use
Agents 04, 14, 18, and 30 may request research through Agent 01. Findings return to the owning agent.

## Steps
1. Restate the exact research question and decision it supports.
2. Search only sources relevant to that question.
3. Prefer primary documentation and current authoritative sources.
4. Separate verified facts, uncertainty, and recommendations to investigate further.
5. Record material version/date constraints.
6. Return concise findings and source links/evidence.
7. Stop once the owning question is sufficiently answered.

## Hard Stop
Research does not make the product, architecture, integration, or release decision.
