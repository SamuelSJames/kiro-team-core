# REAPER Feasibility

## Owner
Agent 13 — Research Agent.

## Shared Use
Agents 21 and 24 consume the findings. They do not replace the research gate.

## Steps
1. Extract every requested REAPER-dependent capability from the validated intake.
2. Research current REAPER support and applicable methods: ReaScript, JSFX, OSC, Web Remote, actions, markers/regions, extensions, project data, and other relevant interfaces.
3. Classify each capability:
   - POSSIBLE
   - POSSIBLE WITH LIMITATIONS
   - REQUIRES WORKAROUND OR CUSTOM EXTENSION
   - NOT CURRENTLY PRACTICAL
4. Record limitations, dependencies, and evidence.
5. Route material product-impacting findings through Agent 01 before mockup/feature approval.

## Hard Stop
Do not approve a REAPER-dependent feature based on assumption.
